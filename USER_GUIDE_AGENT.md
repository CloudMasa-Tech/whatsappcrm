## 1. Logging In & Setting Up Your Workspace

1. Open your web browser (Google Chrome, Edge, or Safari) and go to your company CRM link:  
   👉 `https://crm.yourcompany.com` (or the URL provided by your manager).
2. Enter your work email and password.
3. Once logged in, you will see your **Dashboard** showing your active WhatsApp chats, unread messages, and sales pipeline at a glance.

---

### 2.1 Chat List & Smart Filters
At the top of the conversation list, use quick tabs to organize your work:
* **All**: All incoming WhatsApp chats across the business.
* **Assigned to Me**: Only chats you are personally responsible for.
* **Unassigned**: New inbound WhatsApp inquiries waiting for someone to pick up.
* **Unread**: Chats that have new incoming messages requiring attention.

---

### 2.2 Sending Messages, Files & Voice Notes
* **Text**: Type your message in the bottom input bar and press `Enter` to send.
* **Attachments**: Click the 📎 paperclip icon to upload images, PDF brochures, price lists, or videos.
* **Emojis**: Click the 😊 icon to add emojis.

---

### 2.3 Using AI Smart Drafts (Instant Replies)
Save hours of repetitive typing using the built-in AI:
1. When a customer asks a question, look at the message bar.
2. Click the **"✨ Draft with AI"** button.
3. The AI reads the recent chat context and automatically generates a polite, professional reply.
4. You can edit the text before sending or press `Enter` to send immediately!

---

### 2.4 Quick Replies (Slash Commands `/`)
For answers you type all the time (like greeting messages, bank details, or address links):
1. In the chat box, simply type `/` (forward slash).
2. A menu of saved templates will pop up (e.g. `/pricing`, `/location`, `/thanks`).
3. Click or press `Enter` on the response you want, and it will instantly populate your message box.

---

### 2.5 Leaving Private Internal Notes
Need to leave a reminder for yourself or brief a colleague about a customer?
1. Click the **"Internal Note"** toggle button below the chat box.
2. The box will turn **soft yellow**.
3. Type your note (e.g., *"Customer asked for a 10% discount, waiting for manager approval"*) and send.
4. **The customer will NEVER see this note** — it is only visible to your internal team!

---

### 2.6 Reassigning & Snoozing Conversations
* **Reassign**: Click the agent avatar at the top right of the chat to hand over the conversation to another sales rep or support teammate.
* **Snooze**: Click the ⏰ clock icon if a customer says *"Call me back on Friday"*. The chat will hide from your active list and automatically reappear on the exact date and time you set.

## 3. Managing Contacts & Customer Details

On the right side of every chat, you will see the **Customer Profile Panel**:

### 3.1 Viewing Contact Profiles on the Fly
* See full name, phone number, and conversation history.
* View previous WhatsApp conversations and orders without leaving the chat.

### 3.2 Adding Tags & Custom Attributes
* **Tags**: Click **"+ Add Tag"** to categorize the customer (e.g., `Wholesale`, `Retail`, `Interested in Demo`, `VIP`).
* **Custom Fields**: Update specific attributes like *Budget*, *Company Name*, *Preferred Language*, or *City*.

### 4.1 Creating a Deal from a Chat
1. In any open conversation, look at the right customer panel.
2. Click **"+ Create Deal"**.
3. Enter the **Deal Title** (e.g., *"Annual Subscription"*), **Deal Value** (e.g., *$1,500*), and select the initial stage.

### 4.2 Dragging Deals Across Stages
1. Click **Pipelines** (💼) in the sidebar.
2. Simply drag and drop deal cards from left to right as the customer moves closer to purchasing (*New Lead $\rightarrow$ Proposal Sent $\rightarrow$ Won*).

## 5. Sending WhatsApp Broadcasts

Need to send a new product alert, holiday greeting, or special discount to a group of customers?

1. Go to **Broadcasts** (📢) in the sidebar.
2. Click **"New Campaign"**.
3. Select the target audience using your saved tags (e.g., `VIP Customers`).
4. Type your message and use personalized tags like `Hi {{name}}`.
5. Preview your message and click **"Send Now"** or schedule it for a specific time.

## 6. Team Collaboration & Collision Prevention

You never have to worry about two agents accidentally answering the same customer at the same time:

* **Live Presence Warning**: If your colleague is currently viewing or typing in a WhatsApp chat, you will see their live avatar and a yellow notice:  
  ⚠️ *"Sarah is currently viewing this conversation."*
* **Real-Time Sync**: As soon as a teammate replies, the message updates on your screen instantly.

## 8. Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### ❓ What if a customer messages outside business hours?
If your company has enabled the **AI Auto-Reply** or **Bot Flow**, the system will greet the customer automatically and answer common questions based on your company handbook. The chat will be waiting in your inbox when you log in next morning.

### ❓ How do I search for an old WhatsApp message or contact?
Use the search bar at the top of the **Inbox** or **Contacts** tab. You can search by **customer name, phone number, or keywords** inside previous messages.

### ❓ What does the green status mean?
A green dot means your company's WhatsApp number is online and actively connected.

### ❓ Can I use CloudMasa on my tablet or mobile phone?
Yes! Simply open your mobile browser, go to your CRM URL, and log in. The interface adapts cleanly to mobile and tablet screens.
